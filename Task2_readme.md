# 📊 Facebook Campaign Performance Tracker (Power BI Dashboard)



## 📘 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a one-page overview of Facebook Ad Campaign performance. It tracks key metrics such as impressions, clicks, CTR (Click-Through Rate), total campaign cost, and ROI (Return on Investment). The dashboard enables marketing teams to quickly evaluate ad effectiveness, audience engagement, and return efficiency.

---

## 🧮 Data Fields
The dataset includes:
- `ad_id`, `campaign_id`, `fb_campaign_id`
- `reporting_start`, `reporting_end`
- `age`, `gender`, `interest1`, `interest2`, `interest3`
- `impressions`, `clicks`, `spent`, `total_conversion`, `approved_conversion`

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Highlights

### 1. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- **Total Impressions:** 79M  
- **Total Clicks:** 13K  
- **CTR:** 0.02%  
- **Campaign Cost:** 20.11K  
- **ROI:** -85.46%

These KPIs summarize overall campaign reach, engagement, and cost efficiency. The negative ROI indicates that the campaign cost outweighed the returns, highlighting a need for optimization.

---

### 2. **Top Performing Ads**
A horizontal bar chart compares ads based on CTR, identifying which creatives or campaigns drove the most engagement.

### 3. **Conversions by Age Group**
A stacked column chart shows conversion distribution by age and gender.  
Insight: The **30–34 age group** had the highest approved conversions, with males slightly outperforming females.

### 4. **Campaign Trend Over Time**
A line chart visualizes CTR trends over the reporting period, showing an initial spike followed by a consistent decline — suggesting potential ad fatigue or audience saturation.

### 5. **Relationship Between Impressions and Conversions**
A scatter plot illustrates how impressions relate to approved conversions, sized by campaign spend. The limited dispersion indicates that most campaigns achieved similar engagement levels regardless of spend.

### 6. **Interactive Slicers**
- **Age Group Slicer:** Filter audience performance by demographic segments.  
- **Campaign Selector:** Compare multiple campaign IDs.  
- **Interest Group Slicer:** Explore audience behavior based on ad targeting interests.

---

## 💡 Insights & Recommendations
- **Optimize Targeting:** Focus future campaigns on the 30–34 age group for higher conversion rates.
- **Creative Refresh:** Declining CTR over time suggests the need to rotate or redesign creatives.
- **Improve ROI:** Reassess ad spend allocation and conversion value to enhance profitability.
- **Interest-Based Targeting:** Evaluate which interest segments contribute most to engagement.
